# Help make research assistance work for more people

Contribute a missing method, clearer guidance, a translation or a reproducible bug report. Start with a research need: who needs the change, where they get stuck and what would improve.

## Methods and review

Explain the question and research tradition your method fits. Provide primary references, required materials, expected outputs and appropriate checks. Distinguish reporting completeness from scientific judgment. Use independently written text or compatible licenses and preserve attribution.

## Feedback and changes

Use the repository's issue templates for bugs or method suggestions. Describe the actual behavior and the result you need. Keep private materials, participant information and credentials out of issues and pull requests.

Big Research is an independent third-party project. Contributors do not need an OpenAI affiliation or an official plugin listing.

Code contributors can find setup and verification commands in the [maintainer guide](https://github.com/HaoYangJin/Big-Research/blob/main/.github/maintainers/development.md).
