# Layered assessment

Use general research quality, applicable method checks, then optional venue or reporting overlays. Reporting completeness, artifact functionality, methodological quality and evidence certainty are different judgments. Without a formal rubric, state the question, paradigm and contribution, and explain a transparent working rubric. Interpretive work can be assessed through provenance, context, coherence and rival readings without statistical requirements or an ICLR total.

For full reporting audits retrieve the current primary checklist and cite its individual items. Bundled profiles are bounded summaries/selectors, not complete official forms. Every substantive judgment needs a location and reason; missing material is not_assessable, not a confirmed defect. Declare reviewed/total coverage, selection and confidence. Re-review actual changed materials.

## Stanford Agentic Reviewer

The [technical overview](https://paperreview.ai/tech-overview), inspected 2026-10-16, describes multi-specificity related-work search, selective full-text reading and seven dimensions. Use its search/feedback principles with field-appropriate sources. Experimental soundness applies only to work containing experiments.

Its authors sampled 300 ICLR-2025 submissions, excluded 3 withdrawals, trained a linear combination on 150 and tested on 147. Reported Spearman correlation was 0.42 AI/human versus 0.41 human/human. This does not validate every review, another discipline or this plugin. No Stanford weights, external service, reproduced benchmark or score calibration is bundled. We provide qualitative judgments rather than an unvalidated weighted total.

## Analemma FARS

The [official deployment report](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/analemmaai_ai-llm-research-activity-7431589315215966208-NQBz) describes proposals, code, manuscripts and AI reviews. Useful lessons are inspectable intermediate artifacts, run identity and resource records, with generated work separate from assessment. That report itself describes conference-style AI scores as reference-only for its short-form output. Output volume and another AI's score do not prove scientific correctness.

The [FARS introduction](https://analemma.ai/blog/introducing-fars/) timed out during implementation. Its internal architecture is not treated as verified; no external FARS code is bundled. Revisit the primary source before making additional architecture claims.
