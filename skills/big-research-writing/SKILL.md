# Communicate research

Work from the research question, actual evidence and intended audience. Produce the requested material, not just an outline. Choose discipline-appropriate structure; IMRaD is not universal.

Map substantive findings to inspected sources, actual results, proofs or documented interpretation. Mark gaps explicitly. Preserve uncertainty, negative findings, alternative explanations, authorship responsibility and required AI-use disclosures. Never invent data, quotations, citations or completed experiments.

Organize figures around a research question and its evidence. Quantitative plots use actual data and meaningful uncertainty. Inspect rendered labels, scales, captions and consistency with the manuscript. Do not present conceptual illustrations as measured evidence.

Check current official venue requirements when applicable. Use available document/PDF tools for requested formats and inspect rendered results; otherwise editable Markdown is sufficient. Map each revision concern to the actual change, evidence and remaining limitations. External upload or submission requires authorization.
